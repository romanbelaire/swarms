Let the observed team utility at step $t$ be
\[
\Gamma_t = \sum_j (P_t^j - C_t^j).
\]

Define two scenario axes:
\[
a \in \{\text{conflict-solver},\ \text{neutral},\ \text{conflict-causer}\},
\]
\[
b \in \{\text{others-C},\ \text{others-P},\ \text{others-mean}\}.
\]

The two axes are separable:
\begin{itemize}
  \item The \textbf{others axis} $b$ governs $U_{\text{with}}$: it encodes the \emph{known} current state (what others are doing with agent $i$ present). Since the others observe the team outcome but cannot isolate $i$'s individual role, $U_{\text{with}}$ is indeterminate with respect to~$a$ and depends only on~$b$.
  \item The \textbf{my-role axis} $a$ governs $U_{\text{without}}$: it encodes the \emph{counterfactual} baseline of what the team would achieve if agent $i$ were removed. This depends only on $i$'s role, not on which others scenario is active.
\end{itemize}

Define code-level scalars used by the forced scenario table:
\[
\text{full\_duration} = T \quad (\text{implementation: } T=\texttt{max\_steps\_per\_episode}),
\]
\[
\text{my\_mean}_t^i = \frac{1}{N_t^i}\sum_{\tau \le t} P_\tau^i
\quad
(\text{running mean of observed } P^i \text{ samples; used in logging only}),
\]
\[
\text{my\_P}_t^i = P_t^i,\qquad \text{my\_C}_t^i = C_t^i,\qquad
\text{observed\_util}_t = \Gamma_t.
\]

For each $(a,b)$, define two forced components:
\[
U_{\text{without}}^{(a,b)}(t,i),\qquad U_{\text{with}}^{(a,b)}(t,i).
\]

Using row order
\[
a \in (\text{solver},\text{neutral},\text{causer}),
\]
and column order
\[
b \in (\text{AllC},\text{AllP},\text{AllSame}),
\]
the forced tables are:
\[
U_{\text{without}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 0 & 0 \\
\text{my\_P}_t^i & \text{my\_P}_t^i & \text{my\_P}_t^i \\
\text{full\_duration} & \text{full\_duration} & \text{full\_duration}
\end{bmatrix},
\]
\[
U_{\text{with}}=
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & \text{full\_duration} & \text{observed\_util}_t \\
0 & \text{full\_duration} & \text{observed\_util}_t \\
0 & \text{full\_duration} & \text{observed\_util}_t
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

Per-scenario difference reward is
\[
D_t^{(a,b)} = U_{\text{with}}^{(a,b)}(t,i)-U_{\text{without}}^{(a,b)}(t,i),
\]
which, due to separability, simplifies to
\[
D_t^{(a,b)} =
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & \text{full\_duration} & \text{observed\_util}_t \\
-\text{my\_P}_t^i & \text{full\_duration}-\text{my\_P}_t^i & \text{observed\_util}_t - \text{my\_P}_t^i \\
-\text{full\_duration} & 0 & \text{observed\_util}_t - \text{full\_duration}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

Use factorized state-dependent gates:
\[
u(a \mid z_t^i) = \mathrm{softmax}(g_A(z_t^i))_a,\quad
v(b \mid z_t^i) = \mathrm{softmax}(g_B(z_t^i))_b,
\]
\[
w_{a,b}(z_t^i) = u(a \mid z_t^i)\,v(b \mid z_t^i), \qquad \sum_{a,b} w_{a,b}(z_t^i) = 1.
\]

The final reward used for learning is
\[
R_t^i = \sum_{a,b} w_{a,b}(z_t^i)\,D_t^{(a,b)}.
\]

\paragraph{Math-to-code mapping.}
\[
\Gamma_t \leftrightarrow \texttt{team\_utility},\quad
\text{my\_P}_t^i \leftrightarrow \texttt{own\_p},\quad
\text{my\_C}_t^i \leftrightarrow \texttt{own\_c}.
\]
The tensors \texttt{without\_me}, \texttt{with\_me}, and
\texttt{scenario\_delta = with\_me - without\_me} are constructed directly from these terms.
